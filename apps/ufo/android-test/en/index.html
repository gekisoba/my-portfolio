Android Testers Wanted

The Android test of UFO Clicker is now open. Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay in a closed test for 14 consecutive days before an app can be released publicly. I'd really appreciate your help!

The game

An idle / incremental game where you play as aliens abducting the people of Earth. The progression is inspired by Antimatter Dimensions.

Stack the Invasion Layers

Eight tiers, each producing the tier below. The numbers grow exponentially — even while you're away.

Extinction, Awakening, and beyond

Reach 1.8e308 abductions and humanity goes extinct. Then Awakening, Gravity Layers, a research tree, challenges, and Spacetime Expansion.

A history of UFOs

192 achievements based on real UFO incidents and sightings from 1947 to 2009, each with a short write-up.

Who can join

Android phone owners

Runs on Android 7.0 or later. iPhones can't join, since the test is installed from Google Play.

A Google account

Use the same Google account to join the group and to install from the Play Store.

Can stay for 14 days

If you uninstall or leave the test early, you no longer count toward the 12 testers.

How to join

Join the Google Group

Open the UFO Clicker Android tester group and click "Join group".

Tap "Become a tester"

Signed in with the same Google account, open the test opt-in page and tap "Become a tester". Joining the group alone doesn't sign you up for the test.

Install from the Play Store

Tap "Download it on Google Play" on the same page and install the app. Then just play it now and then for 14 days.

If the Play Store says "Item not found", make sure it's using the same Google account you joined the group with. Right after you become a tester, it can take a few minutes to show up.

The group's member list is hidden from other members. The group only sends test announcements.

Continue from iOS

You can bring your iOS save over to the Android version.

On iOS

Go to Settings → "Copy to Clipboard" and send the text to your Android device (by note, email, etc.).

On Android

Go to Settings → "Paste to Restore" and paste the text. Your abductions and achievements carry over.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No. The test version is free. There are in-app purchases, but you don't need them to play.

What happens after 14 days?

You can keep playing. After the public release, you'll get updates as the same app.

How do I quit?

Uninstall the app and leave the Google Group. But staying for the full 14 days helps a lot!
