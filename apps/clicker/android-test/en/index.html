The game

An idle tycoon where you tap to earn, hire staff and invest — growing a company from one part-timer to galactic scale.

Tap and idle

Tap CLICK to earn, hire staff and invest in departments. Your company keeps earning while you're away.

Time Leap and beyond

Start over with a Time Leap to earn Karma Points for permanent upgrades. Then the Singularity and World Line shifts.

Stock market

Take your company public and trade shares with other players — on the same market as the iPhone version.

Who can join

Android phone owners

Runs on Android 7.0 or later. iPhones can't join, since the test is installed from Google Play.

A Google account

Use the same Google account to join the group and to install from the Play Store.

Can stay for 14 days

If you uninstall or leave the test early, you no longer count toward the 12 testers.

How to join

Join the Google Group

Open the gekisoba Android app tester group and click "Join group".

Tap "Become a tester"

Signed in with the same Google account, open the test opt-in page and tap "Become a tester". Joining the group alone doesn't sign you up for the test.

Install from the Play Store

Tap "Download it on Google Play" on the same page and install the app. Then just play it now and then for 14 days.

If the Play Store says "Item not found", make sure it's using the same Google account you joined the group with. Right after you become a tester, it can take a few minutes to show up.

The group's member list is hidden from other members. The group only sends test announcements, and is also used for gekisoba's other Android app tests.

FAQ

When does the test start?

It started on November 9, 2026. Join the group and you can take part right away.

Can I bring my iPhone save?

Not at the moment; the Android version starts fresh. The stock market is shared with the iPhone version, though.

Does it cost anything?

No. The test version is free. There are in-app purchases, but you don't need them to play.

What happens after 14 days?

You can keep playing. After the public release, you'll get updates as the same app.

How do I quit?

Uninstall the app and leave the Google Group. But staying for the full 14 days helps a lot!
