Now on Google Play

Clicker Company for Android is now available on Google Play. A big thank you to everyone who joined the test. I couldn't have released it without you.

Android Android 7.0 or later / Free
Get it on Google Play

If You Were a Tester

Keep playing

There's no need to uninstall. From now on you'll get the same updates as the public version.

Leaving the test

Open the testing page and tap "Leave the program" to switch to the public version. Leaving the Google Group is optional (I use it for other Android app tests too).

Same stock market as iPhone

On Android you trade in the same market as iPhone players. Save data can't be transferred between them for now.

Bugs and feedback

If you notice anything, please let me know. I'm a solo developer, so I can't always reply, but I read everything.
